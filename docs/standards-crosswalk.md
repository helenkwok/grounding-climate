# Standards crosswalk

In a verifier's terms, Grounding produces factual findings from rechecking evidence: it recomputes figures, runs screens, records a named person's decisions and says what it could not check. That is closest to the agreed-upon procedures of ISO 14064-3:2019 Annex C, which report findings and give no opinion. Grounding gives no opinion and no conclusion at any level of assurance, limited or reasonable. It is not a verification or validation body, its reviewer is not a verifier in the ISO 14064-3 sense, and nothing below is a claim of conformity. Each row names one Grounding feature (with the file and line that does it), one clause of a standard, what that clause asks in our own words, and how the feature meets it. **SUPPORTS** means the feature does what the clause asks of the work Grounding does, or gives a reviewer the evidence the clause asks for. **PARTIAL** means it does part, and the row says which part is missing. Clause numbers are cited; no standard's text is reproduced.

## Open gaps

What Grounding does not do yet, checked against the code on this branch:

- **No materiality.** ISO 14064-3:2019 §5.1.7; ISSA 5000 ¶98. The disclosure 1% and the earth area tolerance are reproduction tolerances, not materiality.
- **No uncertainty statement on any tCO2e figure.** ISO 14064-1:2018 §8.3 and §9.3.1 (p), (q). The building claim is a point value.
- **No market-based Scope 2 beside the location-based figure.** GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance §7.1 and §6.2: California has contractual instruments, so a report would need both totals. For an anonymised site the market-based figure would be the grid-average fallback, with the missing residual mix disclosed.
- **No per-gas reporting.** Scope 2 Guidance §6.7; Corporate Standard ch. 9. The factor is a combined CO2e rate, and the GWP set inside it is not recorded (ISO 14064-1:2018 §9.3.1 (t)).
- **No independent reviewer of decisions.** ISO 14064-3:2019 §8; ISSA 5000 ¶70(c). One named person decides, and per-record browser keys do not prove who.
- **Remote-evidence conditions not recorded.** ISO 14064-5:2026 §7 asks records to state how far remote techniques were used and how well they worked; §6 (p) asks about evidence fabricated before capture, which digests cannot show; §7 asks about equipment calibration, and there is no meter or sensor calibration evidence. Nor is there the no-site-visit justification of ISO 14064-3:2019 §6.1.4.2.
- **No engagement metadata.** ISO 14064-3:2019 §5.1.1 to §5.1.6 and Annex C.4: no engagement type, objective, criteria references, intended users or a stated "no assurance" level in the record.
- **The advice model's calibration has no validity window.** The request is pinned to `jev-1.13.0`, the version the 0.97 threshold was measured on, and an answer from any other version is refused ([`packages/building-mrv/src/advisory.ts:11`](../packages/building-mrv/src/advisory.ts#L11)). What is still missing is a date or condition after which the calibration must be redone. ISO 14064-5:2026 Annex A.2 (h) (model drift).
- **The battery is not an in-scope passport.** Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 Art. 77(1) requires passports for LMT, EV and industrial batteries over 2 kWh from 18 February 2027. The record is one 2 Ah NASA test cell, roughly 0.007 kWh (our estimate, at an assumed 3.6 to 3.7 V nominal; the data give only the 2.7 to 4.2 V test window), and it covers few Annex XIII fields.
- **Ed25519 verification is not strict.** RFC 8032 §5.1.3 and §5.1.7: the libraries accept a small-order public key with a trivial signature, and Node accepts a non-canonically encoded key. Impact is low while every record also needs a valid ML-DSA-65 signature and keys are made per record; it matters if keys are ever tied to an identity.

## What Grounding never claims

- Conformity with ISO 14064-2 or ISO 14064-3, or that a claim was verified or validated under them.
- Compliance with the ICVCM Core Carbon Principles, or that any program or credit is CCP-eligible. The scoreboard checks published data only.
- Assurance under ISSA 5000, limited or reasonable, or work "in line with" it.
- A compliant battery passport under Regulation (EU) 2023/1542.
- A carbon footprint of a product under ISO 14067.
