# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The main reader is an IEEE ClimateChain judge, reading as a climate or carbon verifier: someone handed a reported
climate figure who has to decide whether it can be trusted. They read many Devpost entries, on a laptop, in office or
daylight, with a few minutes for each. The same page is the backdrop of the 3 to 5 minute video. After the hackathon
the reader is a real verifier, auditor or ESG reviewer.

## Product Purpose

ClimateChain shows what a climate claim rests on: the source data, the analysis rerun in the reader's own browser,
each judgement a person made, and a signature (Ed25519 plus post-quantum ML-DSA-65) that stops matching the moment any
of it changes. It works the same way across six kinds of claim: building emissions, energy forecasts, burn scars from
satellites, battery passports, carbon registry records and public disclosures. Success is a judge who, within 30
seconds, can say who it is for, what problem it solves, and has watched a signature break when one number changes.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, honest, calm. The tone of a calibration certificate or a good audit file: plain words, every figure with
its source, limits stated next to the result. Confidence comes from evidence on screen, never from adjectives.

## Anti-references

- Crypto or web3 landing pages: neon on black, tokens, "blockchain makes it true".
- Generic SaaS dashboards: grids of big numbers with small labels.
- Greenwashing ESG brochures: leaves, turbine stock photos, vague green claims.
- Dense academic papers: walls of text, raw hashes up front.

## Design Principles

1. Show, don't claim: every number on the page is computed or checked in the reader's browser, and says so.
2. One story first, then breadth: walk one case end to end before showing the other five.
3. Limits next to results: what was not assessed sits beside what was, at the same visual weight as a footnote, never hidden.
4. Plain words on the surface, detail one click down: hashes, check names and boundaries live in expandable sections.
5. Verified and broken must be unmistakable: the tamper result reads at a glance, in colour and in words.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Status never by colour alone (always a word too). Respects prefers-reduced-motion. Readable at phone
width with a 16px gutter. Keyboard reachable controls with visible focus.
