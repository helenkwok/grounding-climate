Checked in your browser, not on a server.

A climate number arrives on its own.

  1. You see the total. You don't see the meter readings, the emission factor, or the readings someone left out.
  2. You can't tell who decided what about the odd data, or whether anything changed after they did.
  3. Putting it on a blockchain proves a record wasn't changed. It doesn't prove the number was right.

Grounding keeps the evidence, the judgements and the signature together, and rechecks all three in your browser.

One real case

Five steps from meter to signed claim

A real office building from the open Building Data Genome 2 dataset. The question a verifier brings: can this building's Scope 2 figure be trusted, and who decided what about each odd reading?

  1. 1Measure

    2,160 hourly electricity readings from the building's meter, and the grid's emission factor from EPA eGRID 2016 (California, CAMX).

    …

  2. 2Flag

    A screen flags readings that look wrong: exact zeros, long runs of identical values, extreme jumps. A decision model advises on each one. Its advice is a suggestion; it never sets an outcome.

    …

  3. 3A person decides

    The facility energy manager decides each flagged reading. Every decision is tied to a fingerprint of the whole meter series, so if one reading changes, the decisions go stale.

    …

  4. 4Sign

    The result is signed twice: with Ed25519, used widely today, and with ML-DSA-65, a post-quantum signature standard. Both are checked here, in your browser.

    …

  5. 5Try to change it

    Multiply one reading by 20. The page recomputes everything downstream and checks both signatures again.

    Where the evidence is

    Two of the claims have a place

    The building's site and the satellite scene's footprint, with the modelled wildfire risk each records as context. The other four have no location. Select a shape to open its claim.

    Evidence you bring

    Some registries can't be copied. Bring their report.

    Climate Action Reserve and Gold Standard terms don't allow their records to be republished, so Grounding doesn't fetch them. Download a CSV with the registry's own button and choose it here. It is read in your browser, reconciled with the committed ACR records, and kept only as a fingerprint. The signed decisions then go stale, because they were made on different records.

    For AI agents

    Agents use the same checks

    Through WebMCP the page offers its checks to an AI agent in the browser as tools, only those relevant to the open case. Anything consequential still waits for a person. No agent in your browser? The guided audit runs the same tools here.

    What this does not prove

    • Who signed. The signing keys are demo keys made per run. A signature shows nothing changed since signing; it does not yet identify a real organisation.
    • Why data was odd. A person's decision is a recorded judgement, not a confirmed cause. Site records don't confirm why the building's meter read zero.
    • Company figures. A disclosed figure can't be recalculated from public data. It is accepted only as the company's own statement.
    • Registry gaps. The ACR records are a 100-row sample. Ten groups of retirements have no matching issuance in it and are marked for investigation.
    • Satellite area. The burn-scar detector reproduces the reference pixel by pixel, but its burned area is 36% below the reference map's, and that gap is recorded, not explained.

    Who would use it

    • Verifiers and auditors checking a figure before they sign it off.
    • Facility, energy and battery managers recording what they decided about bad data, and on which data.
    • Registries and reporting teams publishing figures that carry their own evidence.

    It runs in an ordinary browser, on open data formats, with no account and no token.
