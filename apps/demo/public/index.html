Evidence the reviewer brings

Add a Climate Action Reserve or Gold Standard report.

Their terms do not allow ClimateChain to copy their records, and CAR's site disallows automated access. Download a report with the registry's own CSV button and choose it here: it is read in this browser, reconciled with the ACR records, and kept in the case as a digest only. The signed decisions then go stale, because they were made on different records.
